Data & Account Deletion for Digest

App: Digest: AI阅读与语音朗读 (Digest: AI Reader & TTS Audio)

Developer: Tianchang Xu (xtc002007@gmail.com)

How to delete all your data in Digest:

  1. In-App Immediate Data Deletion: Open Digest -> Settings -> tap "Clear All Data". This immediately and permanently deletes all SQLite database tables, reading history, saved articles, and cached audio files from your device.
  2. System-Level Deletion: On your Android device, go to Settings -> Apps -> Digest -> Storage & cache -> tap "Clear Storage".
  3. Uninstalling the App: Because Digest uses a 100% Local-First architecture and does not store your reading data on central servers, uninstalling the application permanently deletes all local data from your device.
