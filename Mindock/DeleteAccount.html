Mindock Account & Data Deletion Request

In compliance with Google Play User Data Policy & GDPR Right to Erasure

Warning: Deleting your account is permanent and irreversible. Once deleted, all your cloud synchronization backups, profile metadata, purchased subscription rights, and remaining token balances will be permanently expunged from Mindock servers.

Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted

  • User Account Profile (Email address, unique user ID, display settings).
  • Cloud sync metadata and remote snapshots.
  • Purchased token balances and subscription entitlements.
  • Audit logs associated with your account.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Mindock operates on a Local-First architecture. If you still have the Mindock app installed, your local database (SQLCipher) and notes remain on your phone until you clear application storage or uninstall the application.

Alternative: In-App Deletion

If you have access to the Mindock mobile app, you can delete your account directly:

  1. Open Mindock > Navigate to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the Account & Subscription section.
  3. Tap Delete Account (注销账号) and confirm the prompt.
