Mindock Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Core Privacy Commitment: Mindock is architected on a Local-First, Privacy-by-Default principle. Your private knowledge, personal notes, recordings, and media files reside in your local encrypted storage (SQLCipher / Hardware Keystore) and are never sold, rented, or monetized for advertising.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

  • Local Content & Documents: Text notes, imported images, audio voice memos, and PDFs. These are stored locally in your device's hardware-backed encrypted vault.
  • Account Credentials (Optional): If you choose to register for cloud synchronization and multi-device collaboration, we collect your email address and an encrypted password hash.
  • Google Play In-App Purchase Data: Order tokens and subscription status processed securely via the Google Play Billing Library to credit tokens and activate Pro memberships. We do not store or process payment card details.

2. Permissions and Their Contextual Usage

Mindock strictly adheres to the principle of least privilege. We only request runtime permissions when contextually required by you:

  • Camera (Optional): Only requested when you tap the camera action to scan physical documents, capture whiteboard notes, or snap reference photos.
  • Microphone (Optional): Only requested when you tap the record button to capture voice memos. Transcriptions are processed either via on-device Whisper or privacy-redacted routing based on your explicit policy choice.
  • Photo Picker & Storage: Mindock adopts Android's modern Photo Picker (SAF), meaning Mindock NEVER requests broad storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). The app can only access the explicit files and images you select.
  • Notifications (Optional, Android 13+): Used solely to alert you when background OCR or batch media indexing tasks finish.

3. Generative AI and Third-Party LLM Policy

Mindock allows you to connect to local on-device models, self-hosted gateways, or cloud LLM APIs (such as DeepSeek):

  • No private content is sent to third-party AI services without your explicit knowledge policy (e.g., P0/P1 privacy tiers guarantee local-only processing).
  • When cloud enrichment is chosen, data is sanitized via the local Privacy Gate.
  • In-app reporting mechanisms are provided on all AI messages to flag improper or inaccurate content immediately.

4. Data Retention, Security, and Encryption

  • Encrypted in Transit: All communication between Mindock and the gateway uses industry-standard TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption. Plaintext HTTP traffic is strictly prohibited.
  • Encrypted at Rest: Local databases are encrypted using SQLCipher with AES-256 keys generated and protected by the Android Keystore hardware security module.
  • Auto Backup Exclusion: Authentication tokens and private encryption master keys are explicitly excluded from Android cloud backups to prevent unauthorized extraction.

5. Account & Data Deletion Rights

Under Google Play user data policies and international privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA), you have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Settings > Account Management > Delete Account. Confirming deletion will instantly wipe your account, profile, and server-side subscription ledgers.
  • Web Deletion Portal: If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can submit a deletion request via our dedicated Web Portal: Account Deletion Portal.
